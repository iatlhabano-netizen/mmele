Mmele: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Effective date: 10 October 2026
App: Mmele (Android), package com.tshipiworks.mmele
Who we are (the "controller"): Artful Tenant, trading as Tshipiworks, POSTAL_ADDRESS_PLACEHOLDER, Gabane, Botswana. Contact: CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER.

1. The short version

  • Mmele is a free board game. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no sign-up. Ranked play uses virtual points only: they have no cash value and cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Play vs Computer and Pass & Play work fully offline. Nothing about those games leaves your phone.
  • Play online uses Google Firebase to connect two phones. To do that, Firebase gives your app a random anonymous ID, stores the game room you play in, and (like any internet server) sees your IP address.
  • Usage and crash data (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics): if you leave "Share usage & crash data" on, the app sends anonymous events (for example "match completed: solo, win") and crash reports. No advertising ID is collected. Switch it off at any time on the home screen.
  • Chat in online games is visible only to the two players in that room and is deleted with the room. You can mute, block or report. A report keeps a copy of the reported message for moderation.
  • We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

2. What is stored only on your phone

Data Why Where
Your last chosen mode and difficulty, sound/haptics settings To remember your choices App storage on your device
Games in progress So you can continue playing Device memory

This data stays on your device. The app opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so these settings are not copied to Google's servers or to a new phone. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. What is processed when you use "Play online"

Online play only happens if you choose it. When you create or join a room:

Data Details Purpose Legal basis (EU/UK, Botswana)
Anonymous user ID A random identifier created by Firebase Authentication ("anonymous sign-in"). It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account. To know which player is which in a room and to stop other people making moves for you Performance of the service you asked for (contract); legitimate interest in security
Game room data The 6-character room code, the board variant, the piece sets chosen, the moves played, whose turn it is, connection status (online/offline), resign/draw/rematch offers, and timestamps To run the game between the two players Performance of the service (contract)
IP address and technical data Your IP address and "user agent" information (Android version, device model, Firebase SDK versions), which Firebase receives automatically with every connection To deliver the connection, protect the service from abuse, and for Google to maintain Firebase Legitimate interest in running a secure service

We do not use your IP address to work out your location.

Who can see room data: the other player in your room, and anyone else who uses the app and enters the same room code while it exists (there is no public list of rooms). Rooms contain no names or messages — there is no chat.

3a. Chat, ranked play and tournaments

  • Chat messages (quick phrases, emoji or up to 120 characters of text, filtered for bad words on your phone) are stored under the game room and readable only by its two players. They are deleted when the room is cleaned up (within 24 hours). Chat can be turned off on the home screen.
  • Blocks are stored under your anonymous ID so blocked players cannot join your rooms.
  • Reports store the room code, both anonymous IDs and the reported message. Only the developer (admin) can read them; they are kept up to 90 days.
  • Ranked play stores your rating, virtual points balance, number of games and a points ledger under your anonymous ID. The leaderboard shows ratings with anonymous labels only. Points have no cash value.

3b. Usage and crash data

With "Share usage & crash data" on, Google Firebase Analytics receives anonymous events: app opened for the first time, match started and completed (game, mode, result), tutorial steps, online room created or joined, chat sent (type only, never the text), ranked match, tournament joined and fact shown (category). Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports (stack trace, device model, OS version). The advertising ID is not collected and ad personalisation is disabled. Analytics data is kept for 2 months; crash data for 90 days. Turning the toggle off stops collection immediately.

4. Service provider (data processor)

Online play is hosted on Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, which processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. Game data is stored in the europe-west1 (Belgium) region. Google may process data in other countries where it or its sub-processors operate, using the safeguards in those terms (including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses). See also Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play itself (downloads, updates, reviews) is run by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we receive only aggregated install statistics.

5. How long we keep it

  • Finished game rooms: deleted by the host's app as soon as the host leaves the finished game.
  • Unfinished or abandoned rooms: kept so players can reconnect, then deleted within 60 days of the last activity (rooms idle for 30 days are removed by a clean-up we run at least monthly).

  • Anonymous user IDs: deleted within 60 days of last use (same monthly clean-up), or immediately when you use "Delete my online data".

  • Firebase/Google technical logs: kept by Google for its standard periods under the terms above.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can use Mmele without ever going online. Simply don't choose "Play online".
  • Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage) removes the anonymous ID from your phone; your old rooms can no longer be linked to you.

Delete your online data

  • In the app: Home → About · privacy · licences → Delete my online data. This deletes every room you created or joined from that phone and then your anonymous ID, straight away.
  • By email: write to CONTACT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER with the subject "Mmele data deletion" and, if you can, the room code(s) you used. Because we hold no name or email, we may ask for the room code so we can find the data. We will reply within 30 days.
  • If you never used "Play online", there is no online data to delete.

Your rights

  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your data, and to data portability:
    • EU / EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): you can also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).
    • Botswana (Data Protection Act, 2024): you can complain to the Information and Data Protection Commission (IDPC), Botswana.
    • United States: we do not sell or "share" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use it for targeted ads or profiling.
    • South Africa (POPIA): you may contact the Information Regulator of South Africa.

7. Children

Mmele is a family-friendly board game, but it is not directed at children under 13, and online play is meant for people aged 13 and over. Online play collects no names, no chat and no contact details, only the anonymous technical data above. If you believe a child under 13 has used online play, contact us and we will delete the related data.

8. Security

Connections to Firebase are encrypted (HTTPS/TLS). Database security rules only let the two players in a room change that room, and only in the ways the game allows. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we hold to the minimum needed to play.

9. Changes

If we change how the app uses data (for example, adding a new feature), we will update this page and change the effective date before releasing that version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
