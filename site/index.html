Mmele: Morabaraba of Botswana

The cattle game · Tshameka Mmele

Mmele brings Morabaraba, the traditional cattle game of Botswana and Southern Africa, to your phone. Place your twelve cows, slide them across the board, line up three in a row to form a mill and shoot one of your opponent’s cows, or play Khutlotharo, the quick three-cow triangle game. Play the computer at three levels, pass the phone to a friend, or play online with a six-letter room code, on boards set against the Okavango Delta, Chobe, the Makgadikgadi Pans, the Kalahari and the Tsodilo Hills. No ads, no purchases and no sign-up.

Coming soon to Google Play

  • Two boards

    Classic Morabaraba (24 points, 12 cows) and Khutlotharo (7 points, 3 cows).

  • Play your way

    Against the computer, Pass & Play on one phone, or online with a friend.

  • Private by design

    Offline games never leave your phone. Online play uses only a random anonymous ID.
